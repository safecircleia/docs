SafeCircle is a family safety platform with three parts you use today: a **parent dashboard** at `app.safecircle.tech`, a **parent app** for Android and a **child app** for Android. Together they give parents visibility into risk signals on their child's phone. There is no iOS app yet.

## Dashboard

The parent dashboard runs in the browser at `app.safecircle.tech`. No installation required. It's where you review alerts, manage child profiles and devices, and change your account settings. Live alerts show up while it is open; for push notifications on your phone use the parent app.

## Android (child app)

The child app runs in the background on Android 11 or later. It reads the text visible on screen in five supported messaging apps, analyses it on the phone and sends risk summaries to the dashboard. Nothing is shown to the child.

## Canopy OS

Canopy OS is an experimental Linux distribution being built with SafeCircle protection included. It is at a very early stage and not ready for families. See [Canopy OS](/en/docs/platform/canopy-os/overview).
