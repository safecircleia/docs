An alert is created when the child app detects a risk signal above its threshold. Only `medium` and `high` risk levels create an alert — weaker signals are discarded on the child's device. SafeCircle never sends the message text.

## Risk levels

<Tabs items={["High", "Medium"]}>
  <Tab value="High">
    A strong risk signal. Shown with a **red** badge. Worth a conversation the same day.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Medium">
    A moderate risk signal. Shown with an **orange** badge. Worth a look — it may be a false positive depending on context.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What an alert shows

Open an alert from the list to see its details.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Risk level** | High or medium, plus a **Read** / **Unread** badge |
| **Child** | Which child's device it came from |
| **Source app** | The app where the signal was detected |
| **Confidence** | How sure the model is, from 0 to 100% |
| **Detected** | When it was detected |
| **Categories** | One or more risk labels from the model |
| **AI Reasoning** | A short note on why it was flagged. For now this is a plain summary of the detected categories (for example, *Detected: grooming*). It never quotes the conversation |

## Finding alerts

The **Alerts** page has four tabs:

- **All** — every alert, newest first.
- **Unread** — only alerts you haven't opened yet, with a count.
- **High** and **Medium** — filter by risk level.

If you have more than one child, a **Filter by child** menu appears. Pick **All children** to see everyone again.

A summary panel shows **Total alerts**, **Unread**, **High risk**, **Medium risk** and **Top app** for the current selection.

You can also open an alert directly from the bell in the top bar, which lists the latest five alerts.

## Marking alerts as read

Opening an alert marks it as read. You can also:

- Click **Mark as read** in the alert's details.
- Click **Mark all read** above the list to clear everything unread in the current view.
- Use **Mark all read** in the bell menu.

Read alerts stay in the **All** tab and in the [Timeline](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/analytics-timeline#timeline). Nothing is deleted.

## What to do next

The Alerts page includes a short guide, **What to do next**:

- Start with curiosity, not punishment. Ask what happened before talking about the app.
- High risk alerts are worth a conversation the same day.
- Confidence is a rough indicator, not a probability. Even a lower score can be worth a look.
