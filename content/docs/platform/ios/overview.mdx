There is no SafeCircle app for iPhone or iPad. The child app runs only on Android 11 or later, and no iOS app, beta or release is available.

<Callout type="info">
  We haven't announced a date or a feature list for iOS. When there is something to share, it will be announced on [safecircle.tech](https://safecircle.tech) and in these docs.
</Callout>

## What you can do today

- **Monitor an Android phone.** The [Android child app](/en/docs/platform/android/setup) is the only way to monitor a device.
- **Use the dashboard from any device.** The [parent dashboard](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/setup) is a website, so you can check alerts from an iPhone or iPad browser.
- **Parent app.** The [parent app](/en/docs/platform/parent-app/overview) is Android only too.

## Good to know

- A child's iPhone, iPad or any non-Android device can't be paired or monitored.
- iMessage and other Apple apps aren't covered. See [Known limitations](/en/docs/support/known-limitations).
