SafeCircle is in **private beta** and access is by invite only. This page walks you through everything, in order.

| Step | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your email | Get an invite code |
| 2 | app.safecircle.tech | Create and verify your account |
| 3 | app.safecircle.tech | Wait for an admin to approve you |
| 4 | Google Groups and Google Play | Join the testers group and opt in |
| 5 | Child's Android phone | Install the app and accept the beta agreement |
| 6 | Dashboard and child's phone | Pair the device |

<Callout type="info">
  You need an **Android 11 or later** phone for the child app, and a **Google account** that you use on Google Play.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Get an invite code
    The SafeCircle team sends you an email from `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` with your invite code. There is no public request form.

    - A code looks like `SC_` followed by 21 letters and numbers. It is case-sensitive.
    - The email also includes an **invite link**. Opening it takes you to the sign-up form with your code already filled in.
    - Codes can be limited in the number of times they can be used and may expire.

    <Callout type="warn">
      Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive, and add `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` to your contacts so later emails aren't filtered.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create your account
    1. Go to **app.safecircle.tech**. If you didn't use the invite link, click **Sign up**.
    2. Enter your **Name**, **Invite code**, **Email** and **Password**, then click **Create account**.
    3. Open the verification email from `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` and click the link. You're brought back to the sign-in page. If it didn't arrive, click **Resend email**.
    4. Sign in with your email and password.

    You can also sign up with Google, Discord or Twitter / X. Enter your email and invite code first, then continue with the provider.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Wait for approval
    New accounts have to be approved by the SafeCircle team before you can use the dashboard. Until then you'll see an **Access pending** screen:

    > Your account is awaiting approval. You'll get access as soon as an admin approves your request.

    You don't need to do anything. Come back to **app.safecircle.tech** and sign in again later. Click **Sign out** if you want to leave. If you think it's a mistake, contact the SafeCircle team at **support@safecircle.tech**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Join the beta
    Beta builds are distributed per platform. Pick yours.

    <Tabs items={["Android", "iOS"]}>
      <Tab value="Android">
        Android apps are distributed through Google Play testing, which is only open to members of the **SafeCircle Testers** Google group.

        1. Open the group page: [groups.google.com/g/safecircle-testing](https://groups.google.com/g/safecircle-testing).
        2. Sign in with **the same Google account you use on Google Play** on the phone, and join the group (`safecircle-testing@googlegroups.com`).
        3. Opt in to the testing track for each app you want to test:

        | App | Who uses it | Opt-in link |
        |---|---|---|
        | SafeCircle Parents | Parent's phone | [Become a tester](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.safecircle.parents) |
        | SafeCircle Child | Child's phone | [Become a tester](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.safecircle.child) |
        | SafeCircle AI Playground | Any phone, to try the AI models | [Become a tester](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.safecircle.aiplayground) |

        Once your account is approved you can also do step 2 from the dashboard: go to **Settings › General › Updates**, switch on **Google Play beta updates** and click **Join the group** in the **Join the beta testers** dialog.

        <Callout>
          The Google account matters. Google Play only shows the apps to accounts that are in the group, so use the account that is signed in to the Play Store on that phone.
        </Callout>
      </Tab>
      <Tab value="iOS">
        There is no iOS app yet, for children or for parents, so there is no iOS beta. You can still use the dashboard from any browser, including on an iPhone. See [iOS](/en/docs/platform/ios/overview).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Install the child app
    On the child's phone, open the SafeCircle listing in Google Play and tap **Install**. The listing may take a little while to appear after you join the group.

    The full setup, including the permissions the app needs, is in [Set up the child app](/en/docs/platform/android/setup).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Accept the beta agreement
    The first time you open the app, a **Closed Beta** screen says:

    > You've been invited to test SafeCircle. This build is confidential and not for public distribution.

    Tick both confirmations to continue:

    - **I'm a beta tester and won't share this app**
    - **I understand distribution is prohibited and may result in legal action**

    The agreement means you've been invited as a tester, you won't share the app with anyone else, and you understand that distributing it is prohibited. The app keeps a **CLOSED BETA** reminder in its Settings.

    <Callout type="info">
      Depending on the build, the screen may also ask you to agree to the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**, with links to both documents. You can read them before accepting.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pair the device
    Add a child in the dashboard and generate a pairing code, then scan or enter it in the child app. See [Set up the parent dashboard](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/setup) and [Set up the child app](/en/docs/platform/android/setup).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating beta builds

Google Play delivers beta updates like any other app. Keep automatic updates on, or open the SafeCircle page in Google Play and tap **Update**.

The child app starts in **Basic** (keyword) mode with no download. If you later switch it to an on-device AI model, that model is updated separately: when a newer one is available the app shows **Model update available**. See [Updates](/en/docs/platform/android/updates).

The dashboard updates itself. See [Updates and News](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/updates).

## Leaving the beta

- **Stop receiving beta builds** — leave the **SafeCircle Testers** group at [groups.google.com/g/safecircle-testing](https://groups.google.com/g/safecircle-testing) and switch off **Google Play beta updates** in **Settings › General › Updates**.
- **Stop monitoring** — uninstall the child app, and revoke the device in the dashboard (see [Children and devices](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/children-devices)).
- **Delete your data** — delete your account in **Settings › Profile**. See [Account and security](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/account-security).

## Sending feedback

Your feedback shapes the product. In the dashboard, open the **···** (**More**) menu in the top bar and choose **Report a bug** or **Request a feature**. The page and version you're on are attached automatically, and you can follow your submission afterwards.

You can also email **support@safecircle.tech**. For device problems, include your Android version and phone model.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="The app isn't visible in the Play Store">
    - Confirm you joined the **SafeCircle Testers** group with the Google account that is signed in to Google Play on that phone.
    - Make sure you opted in to the testing track using the link in your invite email.
    - Wait a few minutes after joining, then close and reopen the Play Store.
    - Check that the phone runs Android 11 or later. Older versions can't install the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I joined the group with the wrong Google account">
    Leave the group on the wrong account, then join again with the account used on Google Play. To switch the Play Store to another account, tap your profile picture at the top of the Play Store and pick the account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Invalid invite code"'>
    Check for typos. Codes are case-sensitive and start with `SC_`. Copy and paste the code from the email instead of typing it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invite code was already used or has expired">
    A code can be limited in uses and may have an expiry date. Contact **support@safecircle.tech** with the email address your invite was sent to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invite link didn't fill in my code">
    Paste the code from the email into **Invite code** on the sign-up form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get the verification email">
    Check your spam folder, then click **Resend email** on the **Check your inbox** screen. If it still doesn't arrive, contact **support@safecircle.tech**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm stuck on Access pending">
    Approval is done by the SafeCircle team. Sign in again later. If it has been a while, contact **support@safecircle.tech**.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
