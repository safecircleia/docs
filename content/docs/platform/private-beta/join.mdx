Anyone can create a SafeCircle account and install the Android apps from Google Play. This page walks you through everything, in order.

| Step | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | app.safecircle.tech | Create and verify your account |
| 2 | Google Play | Install the apps |
| 3 | Child's Android phone | Open the app and follow the setup |
| 4 | Dashboard and child's phone | Pair the device |

<Callout type="info">
  You need an **Android 11 or later** phone for the child app, and a **Google account** that you use on Google Play.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create your account
    1. Go to **app.safecircle.tech** and click **Sign up**.
    2. Enter your **Name**, **Email** and **Password**, then click **Create account**.
    3. Open the verification email from `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` and click the link. You're brought back to the sign-in page. If it didn't arrive, click **Resend email**.
    4. Sign in with your email and password.

    You can also sign up with Google, Discord or Twitter / X.

    <Callout type="info">
      If you see **New sign-ups are temporarily closed**, registration has been paused by the SafeCircle team. Try again later, or contact **support@safecircle.tech**.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Install the apps
    Pick your platform.

    <Tabs items={["Android", "iOS"]}>
      <Tab value="Android">
        The Android apps are available on Google Play. Open the listing on the phone and tap **Install**:

        | App | Who uses it | Google Play |
        |---|---|---|
        | SafeCircle Parents | Parent's phone | [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.safecircle.parents) |
        | SafeCircle Child | Child's phone | [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.safecircle.child) |
        | SafeCircle AI Playground | Any phone, to try the AI models | [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.safecircle.aiplayground) |

        <Callout>
          The child app is called **SafeCircle Child** on the phone. SafeCircle Parents is an early version, so expect some rough edges.
        </Callout>
      </Tab>
      <Tab value="iOS">
        There is no iOS app yet, for children or for parents. You can still use the dashboard from any browser, including on an iPhone. See [iOS](/en/docs/platform/ios/overview).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set up the child app
    On the child's phone, open the app and follow the on-screen steps. The full setup, including the permissions the app needs, is in [Set up the child app](/en/docs/platform/android/setup).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pair the device
    Add a child in the dashboard and generate a pairing code, then scan or enter it in the child app. See [Set up the parent dashboard](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/setup) and [Set up the child app](/en/docs/platform/android/setup).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating the apps

Google Play delivers updates like any other app. Keep automatic updates on, or open the SafeCircle page in Google Play and tap **Update**.

The child app starts in **Basic** (keyword) mode with no download. If you later switch it to an on-device AI model, that model is updated separately: when a newer one is available the app shows **Model update available**. See [Updates](/en/docs/platform/android/updates).

The dashboard updates itself. See [Updates and News](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/updates).

## Stopping or leaving

- **Stop monitoring** — uninstall the child app, and revoke the device in the dashboard (see [Children and devices](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/children-devices)).
- **Delete your data** — delete your account in **Settings › Profile**. See [Account and security](/en/docs/platform/dashboard/account-security).

## Sending feedback

Your feedback shapes the product. In the dashboard, open the **···** (**More**) menu in the top bar and choose **Report a bug** or **Request a feature**. The page and version you're on are attached automatically, and you can follow your submission afterwards.

You can also email **support@safecircle.tech**. For device problems, include your Android version and phone model.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="The app isn't visible in the Play Store">
    - Check that the phone runs Android 11 or later. Older versions can't install the app.
    - Update the Play Store, then close and reopen it and search for the app again.
    - Make sure the phone is signed in to a Google account in the Play Store.
    - Open the Google Play link from this page directly on the phone. If Google says the app isn't available for your device or country, contact **support@safecircle.tech** with your phone model and country.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get the verification email">
    Check your spam folder, then click **Resend email** on the **Check your inbox** screen. If it still doesn't arrive, contact **support@safecircle.tech**.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
