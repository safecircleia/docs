SafeCircle is currently in **private beta**. Access requires an invite code sent directly by the SafeCircle team — there is no public request form.

<Callout type="info">
  Looking for the whole journey, including Google Play testing and the beta agreement? See [Join the beta](/en/docs/platform/private-beta/join).
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Receive your invite code
    The SafeCircle team sends you an email containing your invite code. The code format is `SC_` followed by 21 letters and numbers. The email also has an invite link that opens the sign-up form with your code already filled in.

    <Callout type="warn">
      Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive. Add `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` to your contacts to prevent future emails being filtered.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create your account
    1. Go to **app.safecircle.tech** and click **Sign up**.
    2. Enter your name, the invite code, your email address and a password, then click **Create account**.
    3. Open the verification email from `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` and click the link.
    4. Sign in. New accounts must be approved by the SafeCircle team, so you may see an **Access pending** screen until then.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Join the Google Play beta
    The child app is distributed through Google Play's internal testing track. Follow [the Google Play steps in Join the beta](/en/docs/platform/private-beta/join#join-the-beta) before you try to install it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Continue setup
    <Cards>
      <Card title="Set up the parent dashboard" href="/en/docs/platform/dashboard/setup" description="Add a child profile and generate a pairing code." />
      <Card title="Set up the child app" href="/en/docs/platform/android/setup" description="Download and pair the Android app." />
    </Cards>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite code issues

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title='"Invalid invite code"'>
    Check for typos — codes are case-sensitive. Copy-paste directly from the email rather than typing manually.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invite code already used or expired">
    A code can be limited in uses and may expire. Contact **support@safecircle.tech** with the email address you received the invite on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Didn't receive an email">
    Check your spam folder first. If the email is still missing, contact **support@safecircle.tech**.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
