SafeCircle is currently in **beta**, and anyone can create an account. There is no invite code and no approval step.

<Callout type="info">
  Looking for the whole journey, including Google Play testing and the beta agreement? See [Join the beta](/en/docs/platform/private-beta/join).
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create your account
    1. Go to **app.safecircle.tech** and click **Sign up**.
    2. Enter your name, your email address and a password, then click **Create account**.
    3. Open the verification email from `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` and click the link.
    4. Sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Join the Google Play beta
    The child app is distributed through Google Play's internal testing track. Follow [the Google Play steps in Join the beta](/en/docs/platform/private-beta/join#join-the-beta) before you try to install it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Continue setup
    <Cards>
      <Card title="Set up the parent dashboard" href="/en/docs/platform/dashboard/setup" description="Add a child profile and generate a pairing code." />
      <Card title="Set up the child app" href="/en/docs/platform/android/setup" description="Download and pair the Android app." />
    </Cards>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign-up issues

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title='"New sign-ups are temporarily closed"'>
    The SafeCircle team can pause registration. Try again later, or contact **support@safecircle.tech**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Didn't receive an email">
    Check your spam folder and add `no-reply@auth.safecircle.tech` to your contacts. Then click **Resend email** on the **Check your inbox** screen. If the email is still missing, contact **support@safecircle.tech**.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
